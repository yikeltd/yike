# Document 03 — Communication Platform Constitution

**Authority Level:** Highest Communication & Messaging Authority  
**Governance Scope:** In-App Messaging, Direct WhatsApp Routing, Voice/Video Connect, Notifications  
**Status:** Frozen Baseline (Yike V2 Phase 0)  

---

## 1. Messaging & Connect Philosophy

Communication on Yike is built around intent, safety, and velocity. Communication is not a generic social chat; it is a transactional handshake designed to take buyers and sellers from inquiry to closing quickly.

### Principles:
1. **Direct & Unobstructed**: Buyers should reach sellers through their preferred channels (WhatsApp, Call, Platform Chat) without artificial platform lock-in.
2. **Trust-Proportional Access**: Communication privileges scale with seller verification. Higher trust = more direct contact channels unlocked.
3. **Attributed & Safe**: Every interaction is logged to prevent spam, fraud, harassment, and off-platform evasion abuse.

---

## 2. Conversation Lifecycle

$$\text{Inquiry Initiated} \longrightarrow \text{Lead Attribution Logged} \longrightarrow \text{Connect Routing} \longrightarrow \text{Active Dialogue} \longrightarrow \text{Viewing Scheduled} \longrightarrow \text{Outcome Closed}$$

1. **Inquiry Initiated**: Buyer clicks "Contact Agent", "Chat on WhatsApp", or "Schedule Viewing" on a listing.
2. **Lead Attribution Logged**: Yike records buyer contact metadata, listing reference, timestamp, and referral source.
3. **Connect Routing**: The platform routes the buyer via WhatsApp deep-link or platform chat inbox.
4. **Active Dialogue**: Buyer and seller negotiate price, clarify details, and share attachments.
5. **Viewing Scheduled**: Formal inspection date, time, and location are set.
6. **Outcome Closed**: Deal completed or lead archived.

## 4. Contact Sharing & Media Rules

1. **Contact Sharing**: Phone numbers, email addresses, and office locations may be shared freely once a conversation is established.
2. **Attachments & Media**:
   - Supported Formats: JPEG, PNG, WEBP, PDF (document title/deed), MP4 (short video walkthrough).
   - Size Limits: Images (10MB max), Video (50MB max), PDF (20MB max).
   - Media Pipeline: All uploaded media passes through Yike’s automated optimization and malware scanning pipeline before rendering.
3. **Read Receipts & Typing Indicators**: Rendered in platform chat to provide immediate visual feedback and maintain high engagement velocity.

---

## 5. Conversation Moderation & Safety

- **Automated Fraud Screening**: Messages containing flagged keywords (e.g. advance fee requests, untraceable bank accounts, suspicious wire instructions) trigger warning banners for buyers.
- **Harassment & Spam Protection**: Users can block and report abusive accounts with 1 click. Accounts exceeding report thresholds are temporarily frozen pending review.
- **Audit Logs**: All communication metadata (timestamps, response latency, lead source) is audited to maintain seller quality scores.
