# Document 05 — Subscription & Revenue Constitution

**Authority Level:** Highest Commercial & Revenue Authority  
**Governance Scope:** Subscription Tiers, Monetization Architecture, Pricing Strategy, Trust Services Commercialization  
**Status:** Frozen Baseline (Yike V2 Phase 0)  

---

## 1. Commercial Strategy & Pricing Philosophy

Yike’s commercial model is designed to align platform revenue with seller business growth and buyer safety. We monetization value creation, not basic safety.

### Principles:
1. **Never Gate Safety**: Basic verification tools, fraud reporting, and safety guidelines are free for all users.
2. **Monetize Velocity & Capacity**: Subscriptions charge for active listing volume, priority lead delivery, brand visibility, and advanced analytics.
3. **Decoupled High-Value Trust Services**: Physical inspections, legal title searches, and featured promotions are monetized as standalone transaction services.

---

## 2. Subscription Tier Architecture

The Yike commercial framework operates across four distinct tiers: `CORE`, `PRO`, `PRIME`, and `PINNACLE`.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        PINNACLE (Enterprise)                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                         PRIME (High Growth)                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                          PRO (Professional)                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                           CORE (Starter)                               │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### Tier 1: CORE (Starter)

- **Target Audience**: Individual property owners, private car sellers, and new solo agents testing the platform.
- **Business Problem Solved**: Eliminates barrier to entry by enabling individual sellers to market assets online without upfront software costs.
- **Listing Limit**: 5 Active Listings.
- **Business Growth Tools**: Basic listing manager, standard WhatsApp contact button.
- **Trust Benefits**: Basic Email & Phone OTP verification badge.
- **Communication Privileges**: Standard WhatsApp & platform chat routing.
- **Lead Management**: Standard lead notification dashboard.
- **Analytics**: Basic listing view count.
- **Support**: Standard community support.
- **Why It Exists**: Builds top-of-funnel listing inventory and creates a seamless pathway for sellers to upgrade as their business expands.

---

### Tier 2: PRO (Professional)

- **Target Audience**: Active real estate agents, independent auto brokers, and boutique property managers.
- **Business Problem Solved**: Solves listing capacity bottlenecks and provides professional lead tracking to convert inquiries into closed deals.
- **Listing Limit**: 30 Active Listings.
- **Business Growth Tools**: Priority listing placement in category views, custom profile cover, social media share assets.
- **Trust Benefits**: Verified Agent Badge (upon NIN validation), Inspection Priority Queue.
- **Communication Privileges**: Direct Phone Call Connect enabled, instant WhatsApp lead forwarding.
- **Lead Management**: Advanced Lead Manager with lead status tagging (`New`, `Contacted`, `Viewing Set`, `Closed`).
- **Analytics**: 30-day Lead Insights (views, phone clicks, WhatsApp clicks, top locations).
- **Support**: Priority Email & WhatsApp Support ($< 4$ hour response SLA).
- **Why It Exists**: Serves the core revenue-generating agent demographic who require consistent inventory capacity and fast lead turnaround.

---

### Tier 3: PRIME (High Growth)

- **Target Audience**: Established real estate agencies, multi-location auto dealerships, and property management firms.
- **Business Problem Solved**: Solves team management, brand differentiation, and high-volume lead routing across multiple staff members.
- **Listing Limit**: 100 Active Listings.
- **Business Growth Tools**: Featured Agency Storefront, Team Account Seats (up to 5 sub-accounts), Custom Branding, Automated Boost Credits (2/mo).
- **Trust Benefits**: Verified Business / CAC Shield, Physical Office Verified Badge.
- **Communication Privileges**: Unlimited WhatsApp & Direct Call routing, Call Click Analytics, Scheduled Viewing Automation.
- **Lead Management**: Multi-Agent Lead Distribution, Auto-assignment rules.
- **Analytics**: Comprehensive Business Analytics Suite (conversion rates, agent performance leaderboards, market demand comparison).
- **Support**: Dedicated Account Manager ($< 1$ hour response SLA).
- **Why It Exists**: Captures high-margin commercial revenue from growing mid-tier real estate and auto organizations requiring multi-seat operational controls.

---

### Tier 4: PINNACLE (Enterprise & Major Developers)

- **Target Audience**: Major property developers, institutional estate management companies, and national auto franchises.
- **Business Problem Solved**: Provides enterprise project showcases, custom API integrations, and dedicated marketing campaigns for major developments and large vehicle inventories.
- **Listing Limit**: Unlimited Project & Inventory Listings.
- **Business Growth Tools**: Developer Project Showcase Hub, Custom Landing Page Builder, Unlimited Team Seats, National Campaign Co-Op, Featured Homepage Placement.
- **Trust Benefits**: Executive Developer Verification, Dedicated Legal Title Partner Pre-Audit.
- **Communication Privileges**: Virtual Call Center Routing, Premium Dedicated Concierge Lead Desk.
- **Lead Management**: Enterprise CRM API Sync (Salesforce, HubSpot, custom webhooks).
- **Analytics**: Custom Market Intelligence & Price Benchmark Reports.
- **Support**: 24/7 Executive Concierge & Dedicated Technical Lead.
- **Why It Exists**: Solves institutional developers' need for massive buyer reach, enterprise CRM integration, and bespoke project marketing.

## 3. Revenue Stream Strategy

```
                                 YIKE REVENUE STREAMS
                                          │
       ┌──────────────────┬───────────────┴───────────────┬──────────────────┐
       │                  │                               │                  │
┌──────┴──────┐    ┌──────┴──────┐                 ┌──────┴──────┐    ┌──────┴──────┐
│Subscriptions│    │Trust Services│                 │  Promotions │    │ Transaction │
│ (CORE-PINN) │    │(Inspect/Title│                 │(Boost/Feat) │    │ (Escrow/Fin)│
└─────────────┘    └─────────────┘                 └─────────────┘    └─────────────┘
```

1. **Recurring Subscriptions (`CORE`, `PRO`, `PRIME`, `PINNACLE`)**: Predictable baseline SaaS revenue.
2. **Trust Services (Pay-Per-Use)**:
   - Physical Asset Inspection: Paid by buyer or seller per visit.
   - Legal Title Search: Paid by buyer before closing.
   - Fast-Track Moderation: Paid by sellers for instant 15-minute review.
3. **Featured Promotions & Advertising**:
   - Spotlights & Category Banner Ads.
   - Location Boosts (e.g. "Top Property in Lekki Phase 1").
4. **Future Financial Services (Phase 4)**:
   - Escrow Transaction Fee (0.5% – 1.5% per closed transaction).
   - Deposit Verification & Lease Financing Referral Fee.
