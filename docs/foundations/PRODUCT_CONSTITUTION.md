# Document 01 — Product Constitution

**Authority Level:** Highest Authority in Repository  
**Governance Scope:** Engineering, Product, Design, Operations, Commercial, Strategy  
**Status:** Frozen Baseline (Yike V2 Phase 0)  

---

## 1. Mission

To build Africa’s most trusted marketplace platform where high-value transactions—real estate, vehicles, and capital assets—occur with complete clarity, absolute safety, and zero friction.

---

## 2. Vision

To become the default Trust Commerce Infrastructure for emerging markets, where identity, title, condition, and capital move seamlessly between buyers, sellers, agents, and institutions.

---

## 3. Long-Term Vision (10-Year Horizon)

Yike will evolve from an intelligent listing marketplace into a decentralized, verifiable asset registry and transaction protocol across West Africa. Every property title, vehicle history, identity credential, and escrow payment will be verified, recorded, and guaranteed by Yike’s Trust Engine.

---

## 4. Core Values

1. **Clarity Over Volume**: We would rather host 10,000 verified, active listings than 1,000,000 unverified, dead listings.
2. **Safety as Default**: Trust is not an opt-in feature; it is the fundamental physics of our platform.
3. **Progressive Disclosure**: Complexity is earned through intent. Users are guided smoothly from discovery to deal closing without cognitive overload.
4. **Local Depth, Global Rigor**: Designed specifically for the nuances, risks, and communication patterns of the Nigerian market, built to world-class engineering standards.
5. **Neutrality & Integrity**: Yike does not manufacture metrics, invent fake engagement, or compromise moderation for short-term revenue.

---

## 5. Product Philosophy

- **System Thinks First**: The platform anticipates user needs, automatically validates data integrity, and calculates risk scores before presenting options.
- **One Fact, Once**: Data is recorded once in the authoritative source and propagated cleanly. We never duplicate state or present conflicting metrics.
- **Actionable Over Informational**: Every interface view must answer: *"What should I do next?"* Information without clear next actions is visual noise.

---

## 6. Trust Philosophy

- **Trust Must Be Earned Visibly**: Verification is not a binary badge; it is a dynamic spectrum built from verified identity, proof of asset possession, physical inspection, and transaction history.
- **Zero Tolerance for Deception**: Misrepresentation of location, price, ownership, or vehicle condition results in immediate account suspension.
- **Symmetrical Transparency**: Buyers and sellers have mutual visibility into verification status, response rates, and safety records.

---

## 7. Revenue Philosophy

- **Value-First Commercialization**: Revenue is generated by accelerating deal velocity and lowering transaction risk, never by gating basic safety or create artificial friction.
- **Transparent Tiering**: Subscription tiers (`CORE`, `PRO`, `PRIME`, `PINNACLE`) reflect business scale, listing capacity, and operational capability.
- **Decoupled Trust Services**: Inspections, legal verification, and priority moderation are monetized as distinct high-value services.

---

## 8. Design Philosophy

- **Luxurious Simplicity**: Visual excellence achieved through whitespace, refined typography, and purposeful micro-interactions (Stripe/Apple/Linear aesthetic).
- **Typography Over Containers**: Use hierarchy, weight, and layout to structure information rather than heavy borders and dense boxes.
- **Zero Aircraft Cockpits**: Dashboards are clean entry points focused on essential actions, not overwhelming walls of numbers.

---

## 9. Growth Philosophy

- **Trust-Driven Virality**: Growth is fueled by verified sellers receiving higher-quality, high-intent buyer leads via direct WhatsApp and platform channels.
- **Quality Attracts Quality**: High-trust listings attract serious buyers, which in turn attracts top-tier agents, dealers, and developers.

---

## 10. Operational Philosophy

- **Human Guidance Supported by Automation**: Operational staff (reviewers, verifiers, support) work through specialized tools designed for speed, accuracy, and auditability.
- **SLA Discipline**: Moderation queues, verification requests, and disputes operate under strict, enforced time windows.

---

## 11. Decision-Making Principles

1. **Does this increase trust?** If a feature reduces platform safety or transparency, it is rejected.
2. **Does this reduce friction?** If a feature adds unnecessary steps without adding trust or value, it is rejected.
3. **Is it understood in 30 seconds?** If a Nigerian buyer or agent cannot grasp the value immediately, the design is flawed.
4. **Is it sustainable for 5+ years?** Temporary hacks and unsustainable growth tactics are strictly prohibited.

---

## 12. Success Metrics & North Star Metric

- **North Star Metric**: **Completed Trust Transactions (CTT)** — The total number of verified deals closed on Yike where physical/documentary inspection and seller identity were verified.
- **Supporting Metrics**:
  - Lead-to-Connect Conversion Rate
  - Average Time to Verify (Identity & Listing)
  - Active Verified Seller Retention
  - Platform Safety Score (Zero-Fraud Rate)

---

## 13. Company Principles & Rules for Product Decisions

1. **Never Invent Metrics**: Never fake active views, response times, or user counts.
2. **Preserve Baseline Infrastructure**: Platform constitution architecture is frozen. Expand capabilities; do not rewrite working foundations.
3. **Enforce One Fact Once**: Avoid redundant fields or conflicting status definitions across modules.
