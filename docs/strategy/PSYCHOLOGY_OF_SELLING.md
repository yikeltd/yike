## 2. Deep Breakdown by Archetype

### A. Independent Property Agents & Solo Auto Brokers
- **Primary Need**: Lead volume, personal brand credibility, fast deal velocity.
- **Why They Pay**: They upgrade to `PRO` when they hit listing capacity limits (5 free listings) and realize that verified NIN badges increase buyer call volume.
- **Why They Churn**: Fake buyer leads, slow customer support, complex interfaces.
- **Yike Partnership Strategy**: Provide direct WhatsApp lead notifications, verified agent badges, and zero-clutter mobile lead management.

---

### B. Real Estate Agencies & Multi-Lot Auto Dealerships
- **Primary Need**: Team management, multi-seat lead distribution, brand prominence.
- **Why They Pay**: They upgrade to `PRIME` to manage 100 listings, assign leads to sub-agents, and showcase a CAC-verified corporate storefront.
- **Why They Churn**: Lack of multi-user controls or inability to track sub-agent closing performance.
- **Yike Partnership Strategy**: Multi-seat dashboard, team lead distribution rules, and monthly performance reports.

---

### C. Major Real Estate Developers
- **Primary Need**: Institutional brand positioning, high-volume project marketing, CRM integration.
- **Why They Pay**: They subscribe to `PINNACLE` for unlimited project listings, developer showcase hubs, custom lead API webhooks, and national campaign placement.
- **Why They Churn**: Impersonation by unverified agents posting their developments with unauthorized prices.
- **Yike Partnership Strategy**: Exclusive Developer Verification shield that flags and removes unauthorized duplicate listings automatically.

---

### D. Private Individual Sellers & Landlords
- **Primary Need**: Sells one property or vehicle without being harassed by unscrupulous middle-men.
- **Why They Pay**: They purchase pay-per-use Inspection Reports or Featured Boosts to close their single deal quickly.
- **Why They Churn**: N/A (One-off transaction mindset).
- **Yike Partnership Strategy**: Free `CORE` listing with simple paid upgrade add-ons for instant visibility.
