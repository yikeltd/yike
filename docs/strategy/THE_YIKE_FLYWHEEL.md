## 2. Explanation of Every Connection

### Connection 1 → 2: More Trust → More Buyers
When Yike enforces NIN identity checks, CAC business validation, and physical asset inspections, buyers experience zero fraud and transparent pricing. Trust converts casual visitors into confident, active buyers.

### Connection 2 → 3: More Buyers → More Conversations
A higher volume of confident buyers leads directly to an increase in high-intent buyer inquiries (WhatsApp direct chats, phone calls, and scheduled viewings).

### Connection 3 → 4: More Conversations → More Deals
High-intent, attributed conversations between verified buyers and sellers result in faster deal closings with minimal drop-off.

### Connection 4 → 5: More Deals → More Reviews
Closing deals triggers post-transaction review prompts. Satisfied buyers leave verified ratings and detailed feedback on seller profiles.

### Connection 5 → 6: More Reviews → Better Reputation
Accumulated verified reviews build public seller reputation badges, establishing an authoritative directory of top-tier agents, dealers, and developers.

### Connection 6 → 7: Better Reputation → More Sellers
Professional sellers observe that verified peers close more deals on Yike. To remain competitive and build their own brand reputation, top sellers join the platform.

### Connection 7 → 8: More Sellers → More Revenue
As professional sellers join and expand their active inventory, they upgrade to paid SaaS subscriptions (`PRO`, `PRIME`, `PINNACLE`) and purchase featured promotions and trust add-ons.

### Connection 8 → 9: More Revenue → Better Trust Infrastructure
Reinvested subscription and transaction revenue funds expanded field inspector networks, legal partner coverage, automated verification APIs, and faster moderation SLAs.

### Connection 9 → 1: Better Trust Infrastructure → More Trust
Upgraded trust tools and faster operational turnaround raise the baseline level of platform security, re-igniting the flywheel at higher velocity.
