## 1. Executive Summary: The Nigerian Asset Buyer Mindset

Purchasing real estate or vehicles in Nigeria is not merely a financial transaction; it is a high-stakes emotional event governed by **paralyzing fear of capital loss**, **cultural negotiation norms**, and **collective family decision-making**.

Traditional classified marketplaces fail because they treat property and car buying like e-commerce impulse purchases (e.g. buying shoes or electronics). High-value asset transactions require an entirely different psychological playbook grounded in behavioral economics.

### A. Fear of Capital Loss & Skepticism
- **Behavioral Pattern**: Nigerian buyers operate with baseline distrust. They assume every online listing is potentially fraudulent until proven otherwise.
- **Yike Mechanism**: Surface multi-tiered Trust Badges (`NIN Verified Agent`, `CAC Verified Business`, `Yike Inspected`) directly on hero components to immediately lower heart rate and skepticism.

---

### B. Relational Negotiation & Price Flexibility
- **Behavioral Pattern**: Fixed-price tags on high-value assets generate friction. Nigerian buyers view negotiation as an essential relationship-building exercise to gauge seller legitimacy.
- **Yike Mechanism**: Present transparent price anchors while providing instant "Contact Agent on WhatsApp" buttons to initiate direct negotiation.

---

### C. WhatsApp & Direct Phone Call Primacy
- **Behavioral Pattern**: Email is viewed as cold and slow. WhatsApp and direct phone calls are the default channels for establishing trust, requesting additional photos/videos, and arranging physical meetings.
- **Yike Mechanism**: Optimize the entire inquiry flow around 1-click WhatsApp deep-linking with pre-filled property reference text and automated lead attribution tracking.

---

### D. Collective & Family Decision Dynamics
- **Behavioral Pattern**: Real estate and vehicle purchases are rarely made in isolation. Spouses, extended family members, legal advisers, and trusted mechanics are consulted before deposit payment.
- **Yike Mechanism**: Enable easy social sharing links, downloadable PDF inspection summaries, and shareable verified listing cards optimized for WhatsApp group forwarding.

## 3. Causes & Mitigation of Funnel Abandonment

| Abandonment Trigger | Root Psychological Cause | Yike System Mitigation |
|---------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| **Forced Account Signup** | Privacy fear & friction | **Lazy Registration**: Guest browsing permitted; OTP required only on lead dispatch. |
| **Missing Listing Specs / Pricing** | Suspicion of bait-and-switch | **Strict Moderation SLA**: Mandatory price, location, and amenity fields required for publishing. |
| **Unresponsive Sellers** | Feeling ignored / wasted effort | **Seller Response Rate Badges**: Surface response latency; deprioritize uncommunicative agents. |
| **Unclear Property Title** | Fear of land grabbers / demolition | **Legal Verified Badge**: 1-click ordering for official C of O and Governor’s Consent title searches. |
